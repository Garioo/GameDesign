River · round 5
  • Dates you can see. Every title says its dates, for example “Fri 25 Sep – Wed 7 Oct · 9 workdays”. Hover or select a piece of work and its days light up in the date column, with dashed start and end lines across the plan. The highlight follows you while you drag.
  • White and calm. One light theme with neutral ink. Day lines are gone, only week lines and weekend bands are left, and meetings sit in the date column so no line runs through titles.
  • Text first. A plainer typeface for titles and details, bigger meta text, and titles sit on white above every line.
  • Readable. Each piece of work is a thin line for its duration, with the title written horizontally beside it at full width. Titles are never squeezed or cut off.
  • Less on the plan. The plan shows only pieces of work (parent tasks). Their small tasks are steps inside them. Click a title to see and tick off the steps.
  • Tasks without a parent stay off the plan. See them under “small tasks” in each column header and pin one if it deserves a place.
  • You work through steps in Today. Ticking one fills its parent's progress bar.
  • Drag a title or line to reschedule, and drag the bottom of the line to change its length.
