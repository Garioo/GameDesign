River · round 7
  • Boards and their categories. Each board header has a ⌄ categories button that opens the board to show its categories, the same as the current Gantt. Each category has its own dates and progress. Drag it to move it, drag its bottom end to change its length, or click it to see its tasks. “Expand all” opens every board.
  • Only boards have dates. Each board is a tinted lane that runs down to its end date. The coloured bar at the bottom is its finish line. Drag it to move the end date, or drag the “Starts” tag at the top to move the start.
  • The solid spine shows the time a board has left. The faded part above the now line is time already spent.
  • Tasks have no dates. Each board shows what's in progress on a card at today's position. Click a board to open all its tasks, grouped by stage, and add one with a single line.
  • Milestones and meetings stay on the timeline because they have dates.
